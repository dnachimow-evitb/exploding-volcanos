The Story

It started with splashing.

One ordinary night, I sat on the edge of the tub with my guitar while my two boys — Noah and Max — did what kids do: splash, shriek, and turn bathwater into weather. I started strumming along, narrating the chaos as it happened. When the water heaved over the side, I called it what it obviously was: an exploding volcano.

No studio, no band, no second take — just my phone catching a loud, rough, off-the-cuff jam, my guitar, and two kids as pure forces of nature yelling “Look, Daddy, look!”

It was the kind of fleeting moment I'd normally lose to time. This one, I didn't.

The Journey

From a rough take to the real thing.

Here's the part I didn't plan. Noah and Max kept asking to hear “the song” — like it was any other track on Spotify. But all I had was a rough, rambling 8-minute cut on my phone. So, half as a joke, I fed it to AI to see what it would do.

What came back blew me away. A couple of years ago this is where the moment would've ended — a clip buried in my camera roll. Instead, that messy recording went in one end, and a song I'm genuinely proud of came out the other.

The Companion Game

Splash it. Fill it. Blow it up.

I built this so my boys — and yours — can play the song. Help Noah splash floating bath toys into the volcano hoop. Fill it to the brim and it ERUPTS — toys rain back into the tub for the next round. Ten rounds of escalating, joyful chaos, all scored to our song.
