The Story

It started with splashing.

One ordinary night, I sat on the edge of the tub with my guitar while my two boys, Noah and Max, did what kids do: splash, shriek, and turn bathwater into weather. I started strumming along, narrating the chaos as it happened. When the water heaved over the side, I called it what it obviously was: an exploding volcano.

No studio, no band, no second take. Just my phone catching a loud, rough, off-the-cuff jam, my guitar, and two kids as pure forces of nature yelling “Look, Daddy, look!”

It was the kind of fleeting moment I'd normally lose to time. This one, I didn't.

The Journey

From a rough take to the real thing.

Here's the part I didn't plan. Noah and Max kept asking to hear “the song”, like it was any other track on Spotify. But all I had was a rough, rambling 8-minute cut on my phone. So, half as a joke, I fed it to AI to see what it would do.

What came back blew me away. A couple of years ago this is where the moment would've ended: a clip buried on my iPhone. Instead, that messy recording went in one end, and a song I'm genuinely proud of came out the other.

The Jam

Me on guitar, my boys splashing, narrating it live. Raw, unpolished, of-the-moment.

The AI

The Song

Out came a produced track with a hook my kids now shout: “This is the end, this is the end.” Pow! Pow!

The Game

Splash it. Fill it. Blow it up.

I built this so my boys, and yours, can play the song. Help Noah splash floating bath toys into the volcano. Get them all in and it ERUPTS, and a new toy joins the tub for the next round. Ten rounds of escalating, joyful chaos, all scored to our song.

  • Two ways to throw. Push: press and hold, then let go. Swipe: flick the toy at the volcano and bank it off the wall for double points.
  • A snowballing collection. Every round adds a new toy. Earn coins by playing and pick your own lineup from 39 toys.
  • Watch out for Max. Your little brother swats shots out of the air.
  • Grab the hairball for bonus points and extra time. Gross = good.
  • Badges and players. 12 badges in bronze, silver and gold, and up to four players on one phone, each with their own toys.
  • Scored to the song. Build, overflow, erupt, reset, again.
