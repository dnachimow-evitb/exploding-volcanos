Exploding Volcanoes in the Bathtub

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea for a new bath toy? Email dnachimow@gmail.com. Please say what device you are using and what happened.

How do I play?

Help Noah splash the bath toys into the volcano before the clock runs out. Get them all in and the volcano erupts. Tap How To Play on the start screen for a walk-through with pictures.

  • Push: press and hold a toy, then let go to throw it.
  • Swipe: flick a toy toward the volcano.
  • Easy shows hints. Hard hides them.

How do I get more toys?

Win rounds and earn badges to collect coins, then open My Toys on the start screen to buy toys and choose your lineup. Coins only come from playing. Nothing in the game costs real money.

Can more than one child play?

Yes. Tap the player name at the top left of the start screen to add up to four players. Each one has their own toys, coins and badges.

There is no sound

Check the speaker button at the top right of the game, the volume buttons, and the silent switch on the side of the iPhone.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. It is not backed up to an account, so deleting the app erases it. Progress in the iPhone app and on the website are kept separately.
